Add Onward to your home screen

Onward works like an app: one tap to open, full screen, and it keeps working without a signal (except for live searches).

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Open mightywaters.github.io/onward in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow pointing up). On iPhone it's at the bottom; on iPad it's at the top.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.

Hands busy? Say "Hey Siri, open Onward" after it's installed, or use Voice Control: "Tap Share", "Tap Add to Home Screen".

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app, then Install.

Computer (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Open the link.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the browser menu and choose Install Onward.
